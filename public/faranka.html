Frequently asked questions

What is Faranka?

An Android app that turns bank SMS and receipts into clear spending records — budgets, categories, and trends, on your device.

Which banks are supported?

Awash, CBE, Bank of Abyssinia, and Telebirr, with more coming.

Is my data stored in the cloud?

No by default — parsed and kept on-device. No analytics or ads. Optional encrypted Pro backup.

How do I get started?

Download the APK, sign in with Google, and grant SMS permission once.

Can I edit categories?

Yes — rename, merge, or create categories anytime.
