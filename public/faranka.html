FAQ

Common questions

Everything you need to know about Faranka.

Q.01

What is Faranka?

Faranka is an Android app that reads your bank SMS messages and receipts, then automatically organizes them into a clear picture of your spending — budgets, categories, and trends, all on your device.

Q.02

Which banks are supported?

Faranka currently supports Awash Bank and Commercial Bank of Ethiopia (CBE), with more banks coming.

Q.03

Is my data stored in the cloud?

By default, no. Your bank messages and spending data are processed and kept on your device. Cloud features — like backup and receipt verification — are optional and only ever tied to the account you explicitly sign in with. Skip them and nothing is uploaded. See the full privacy breakdown.

Q.04

How do I get started?

Download the APK, install it on your Android device, and grant SMS permission once. Faranka will find your bank messages and let you choose how many to import.

Q.05

Can I edit categories?

Yes. Faranka automatically assigns categories based on who you paid, but you can rename, merge, or create new ones at any time to match your system.

Q.06

What does it mean Faranka automatically categorizes?

Faranka extracts a reason from every bank message — the name of the person, business, or service you paid. Every transaction is automatically categorized based on it: Faranka compares the reason against categories it has seen before and assigns a match, or creates a new category if none exists.

Q.07

What does each transaction show?

Every transaction shows the amount, date, counterparty name, bank, and category. Tap any transaction to see the full receipt details, edit the category, and view the original SMS.

Q.08

Does Faranka track me or sell data?

No. The app ships with no analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking SDKs and nothing is sold. Check the permissions on your phone, or audit the APK with an independent tool like Exodus Privacy.

Privacy & data

Privacy, spelled out.

Faranka reads bank SMS, so the first question is always “what happens to my data?”. Here is the answer — and where to check it for yourself.

Local by default

Starts and stays on your phone

Messages are parsed on-device and kept in a local database (Drift). Everyday use needs no account, no sign-up, and no upload.

No trackers, no ads

Nothing measured, nothing sold

The app ships without analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking SDKs, and nothing is measured, sold, or shared with third parties.

Optional cloud

Only if you ask for it

Sign-in, cloud backup, and receipt verification are opt-in, tied to your own Google account. Skip them and everything stays on-device.

Protection

Biometric app lock

Optional fingerprint or face unlock keeps your numbers private on the phone itself — verified locally, not in the cloud.

Stays on the device
  • Imported SMS and transactions — parsed and kept in the on-device database.
  • Categories, budgets, and settings — local preferences, on your phone.
  • Biometric unlock — the check happens on the device, nothing is shared.
Uses the internet only when it needs to
  • Update check — looks up the latest release version, and nothing else.
  • Sign-in and subscription — optional Google Sign-In for the paid features that need it.
  • Cloud backup — a copy you explicitly send to a folder only your account can read or delete.
  • Receipt details — when you open a receipt link, the app fetches its contents to show the particulars.
